Econ question of the week

Why should a penalty taker sometimes shoot straight down the middle?

It's a game between kicker and keeper. If the kicker always picks a side, the keeper learns it and dives there. The best strategy is to mix it up so the keeper can't predict anything, a mixed strategy Nash equilibrium. Studies of pro penalty kicks find players randomize almost exactly how the theory says they should.
